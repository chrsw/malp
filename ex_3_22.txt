Exercise 3.22  Sign extend the two-digit hex number 0x88 to a four-digit
hex number.


Solution 3.22
0x88 = 10001000
xxxx xxxx xxxx xxxx xxxx xxxx 10001000
Assuming 0x88 is a two's complement number then we simply extend the 8-
bit MSB to the rest of the word:

1111 1111 1111 1111 1111 1111 10001000
