Exercise 1.6  Identify a kitchen appliance that contains a control unit that issues
control signals.


Solution 1.6
A microwave has a control unit that controls a display, fans, a magnetron and a plate
motor. The control signals from the microwave control unit controls fans speed, digital
displays, a magnetron for high frequency microwaves, fans to cool or distribute air,
signals to deliver power to the microwave antenna, etc.
