Exercise 2.1  Using Apprendix A, translate each of the following pseudocode
expressions into MIPS assembly langauge:

(a) t3 = t4 + t5 - t6;
(b) s3 = t2 / (s1 - 54321);
(c) sp = sp - 16;


Solution 2.1


(a)
sub $t5, $t5, $t6
add $t3, $t4, $t5

(b)
li $t0, 54321
sub $s1, $s1, $t0
div $s3, $t2, $s1

(c)
sub $sp, $sp, 16
