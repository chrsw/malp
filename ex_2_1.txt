Exercise 2.1  Using Apprendix A, translate each of the following pseudocode
expressions into MIPS assembly langauge:

(a) t3 = t4 + t5 - t6;
(b) s3 = t2 / (s1 - 54321);
(c) sp = sp - 16;
(d) cout << t3;
(e) cin >> t0;
(f) a0 = &array;

Solution 2.1


(a)
sub $t5, $t5, $t6
add $t3, $t4, $t5

(b)
li $t0, 54321
sub $s1, $s1, $t0
div $s3, $t2, $s1

(c)
sub $sp, $sp, 16

(d)
li $v0, 1
move $a0, $t3
syscall

(e)
li $v0, 5
syscall
move $t0, $v0

(f)
        .data
array:  .space 4096
la $a0, array
