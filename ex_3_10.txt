Exercise 3.10  Convert the decimal number -35 to an 8-bit two's complement
binary number

Solution 3.10
Two's complement polynomial expansion from Section 3.8:
N = -d7 * 2^7 + d6 * 2^6 + d5 * 2^5 + .... + d1 * 2 + d0
To do the conversion we follow the method from Section 3.9:
35 in base 10 is 00100011 in base 2
Converting 00100011 to two's complement:
Start with the positive binary value:
00100011
Invert it:
11011100
Add one:
11011100
     + 1
--------
11011101
