Exercise 1.7  What is an `algorithm`?


Solution 1.7
An algorithm is a step-by-step solution to a problem. In terms of digital
computation, a algorithm can be written as a list of computer instructions.
Given a problem and inputs or initial conditions, the algorithm will end up
with a solution when executed on a computer. Algorithms are ranked by their
efficient use of resources like computation time and memory. Algorithm
performance with respect to the size of their inputs can be expressed with
big-O notation, short for "on the Order of".
