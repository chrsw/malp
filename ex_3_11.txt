Exercise 3.11  Convert the decimal number -32 to an 8-bit two's complement
binary number

Solution 3.11
Two's complement polynomial expansion from Section 3.8:
N = -d7 * 2^7 + d6 * 2^6 + d5 * 2^5 + .... + d1 * 2 + d0
To do the conversion we follow the method from Section 3.9:
32 in base 10 is 0010000 in base 2
Converting 00100000 to two's complement:
Start with the positive binary value:
00100000
And invert it:
11011111
Add one:
11011111
     + 1
--------
11100000
