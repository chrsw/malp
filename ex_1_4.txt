Exercise 1.4 Explain the difference between the IR and the PC.


Solution 1.4
IR is Instruction Register and contains the bit contents of the currently
executing instruction.
PC is the Program Counter and it contains the address of the next instruction
that will execute, unless changed by the currently executing instruction.
The CPU fetches the next instruction to execute when it's time to execute
the next instruction, from the address in the PC. The instruction is
fetched from that addressed and placed into the IR.
While the instruction is executing, the contents of the instruction, like the
opcode, parameters and immediate data are encoded in the IR.
